# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owner / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Product Owner or Business Sponsor is accountable for the business value and priority of the work. They help ensure that the project remains aligned to strategic goals, customer needs, and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define target outcomes, scope priorities, and business value
- Approve major trade-offs among cost, timeline, and feature scope
- Partner with project and product leaders on decision-making
- Validate that delivered work solves the intended problem

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works closely with Product Managers to translate strategy into prioritized scope
- Collaborates with Project Managers on timelines, approvals, and dependencies
- Provides guidance to Developers and Engineering Managers on business intent and trade-offs
- Escalates issues that affect strategic objectives or stakeholder commitments

### Goals
- Maximize return on investment and business impact
- Maintain alignment between delivery work and organizational priorities
- Ensure clear sponsorship and accountability for project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates
- Prioritization decisions and trade-off reviews
- Executive briefings and milestone approvals

---

## Delivery Lead / Project Manager

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead coordinates execution across teams, turning plans into day-to-day delivery momentum. This role expands the project management function by focusing on dependencies, planning quality, and execution health.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate across teams to manage sequencing, dependencies, and blockers
- Maintain the working plan, milestones, and delivery health metrics
- Help teams resolve cross-functional coordination issues
- Ensure risks and decisions are visible to stakeholders

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Project Managers to align delivery plans with program governance
- Collaborates with Developers and Engineering Managers to confirm team capacity and constraints
- Partners with Product Owners to surface delivery trade-offs and resourcing risks
- Communicates progress and risks to Stakeholder Liaisons and leadership

### Goals
- Keep delivery moving without unnecessary churn
- Reduce handoff delays and coordination gaps
- Maintain a clear path from planning to execution

### Typical Communication
- Delivery standups and dependency reviews
- Status reporting and risk escalation updates
- Coordination with cross-functional partners and sponsors

---

## Engineering Manager / Team Lead

### Role Summary
The Engineering Manager or Team Lead ensures the engineering organization can deliver effectively. This role focuses on execution quality, team capacity, and technical decision support while protecting the team from avoidable delivery risk.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity, work flow, and execution priorities
- Support technical planning and implementation trade-offs
- Coach engineers on quality, delivery expectations, and collaboration norms
- Monitor risks to engineering efficiency and escalate blockers

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Partners with Developers on work planning and technical feasibility
- Coordinates with Product Managers and Product Owners on priority adjustments and delivery constraints
- Aligns with Project Managers and Delivery Leads on scheduling and dependency management
- Supports PMs and leadership with risk and readiness information

### Goals
- Maintain sustainable engineering throughput
- Protect quality and delivery predictability
- Ensure technical work is well-scoped and achievable

### Typical Communication
- Planning sessions and sprint reviews
- Technical risk discussions and dependency updates
- Check-ins with project leadership and functional partners

---

## Risk and Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
The Risk and Compliance Partner monitors risk exposure, governance requirements, and policy alignment during project execution. This role ensures the project remains compliant with internal standards and risk thresholds.

### Responsibilities
- Identify project risks, dependencies, and control gaps
- Define escalation paths and mitigation steps for high-impact issues
- Review key decisions against governance, compliance, or policy requirements
- Support contingency planning for operational and strategic risks

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Project Managers and Delivery Leads to track risk status and escalation timing
- Collaborates with Product Owners on risk trade-offs related to scope, quality, or timelines
- Provides input to Engineering Managers and Developers on constraints that affect delivery safety
- Advises stakeholders and leadership on threshold breaches or critical issues

### Goals
- Reduce uncertainty and exposure to avoidable project failure
- Improve visibility into high-impact issues and mitigation plans
- Support governance without slowing delivery unnecessarily

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and mitigation checkpoints
- Governance updates and policy exception discussions
- Escalation communication to leadership and sponsors

---

## Stakeholder Liaison / Communication Lead

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Liaison or Communication Lead ensures that the right audiences receive timely, relevant, and consistent information about project progress, decisions, and risks. This role helps maintain trust and alignment across stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate updates for sponsors, partners, and cross-functional stakeholders
- Tailor communication to different audiences and decision needs
- Surface stakeholder concerns and maintain alignment on expectations
- Support issue escalation and cross-team communication workflows

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Partners with Project Managers and Delivery Leads on progress updates and issue communication
- Works with Product Owners to align stakeholder messaging to business priorities
- Helps Engineering Managers communicate technical constraints or delivery impacts
- Supports leadership visibility without overloading teams with ad hoc requests

### Goals
- Keep stakeholders informed and aligned
- Reduce confusion caused by inconsistent or late communication
- Promote stakeholder confidence in project execution

### Typical Communication
- Executive summaries and stakeholder briefings
- Decision logs and milestone updates
- Cross-functional communication plans and outreach

---

## Change Manager / Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
The Change Manager or Adoption Lead helps teams and stakeholders adapt to new processes, tools, or ways of working introduced by the project. This role connects implementation work to sustainable adoption and long-term value realization.

### Responsibilities
- Identify adoption impacts for users, teams, and stakeholders
- Coordinate training, communication, and rollout support
- Monitor change readiness and transition risks
- Partner with leadership to reinforce desired behaviors and operational practices

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Coordinates with Product Owners to ensure changes support business intent and adoption outcomes
- Collaborates with Project Managers and Delivery Leads to align rollout timing with project milestones
- works with Engineering Managers and Developers to assess readiness and support implementation changes
- Helps Stakeholder Liaisons communicate adoption impacts effectively

### Goals
- Improve adoption and sustained value realization
- Reduce disruption during process or tool changes
- Strengthen confidence in new ways of working

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and rollout plans
- Training and stakeholder readiness updates
- Adoption checkpoints and success reviews

---

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The project management process should explicitly define ownership, accountability, and communication paths for each role to improve clarity and execution quality.
